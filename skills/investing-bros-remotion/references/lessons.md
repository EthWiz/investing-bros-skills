# Lesson videos (crypto course, 16:9)

Moved from the skill's SKILL.md (no rule changed); the scripts are listed there.

The template's second composition, `Lesson` (1920×1080, 30 fps, `src/lesson/`), plays a lesson's joined takes with captions, strips, an agenda and progress marker, and the lesson's own scenes. The cut and the lesson's config belong to `investing-bros-crypto-course` (its video reference has the workflow and layout); this skill owns the composition and three scripts:

A scene is a component listed by name in `src/lesson/scenes.ts` and declared in `src/lesson/scenes.json` (its mode, the anchors and content keys it reads, its images); the two must name the same scenes. It gets `{at, start, end, content, assets}`, and every word it shows comes from `content`, never from the component. Reusable scenes sit in `src/lesson/` (`terms`: a heading and terms that appear one by one; `double-spend`: one coin, a sender and two recipients, as `scenario: "copy"` (the coin is copied and both get it, then an X) or `scenario: "ledger"` (the bank's ledger splits into everyone's copy and the second payment is refused), in 16:9 and 9:16; `growth-bars`: how much each asset grew, as bars on one linear scale, each rising on its name and showing the multiple Amitai says (`said`), in 16:9 and 9:16; `price-drop`: a fall from a peak on a price chart with no prices on the axes, the line dropping to `(100 - percent)%` of the peak's height with an arrow and a bracket, in 16:9 and 9:16; `conflict`: two computers hear two payments of one coin in a different order, their ledgers part (≠), then the question and a short chain as the way out; `mining`: a simplified mining setup, rigs racing on the puzzle, the winner's piece sealing the page, the others checking it and each getting a copy, the second payment refused; `chain`: linked pages with fingerprints, an old page erased, its print changing and the link breaking, then the network's copies agreeing and growing; `private-key`: the key opens a safe on the coins, is lost, the safe locks and the helpdesk gets an X; all four in 16:9 and 9:16, their drawings shared in `blocks.tsx`), a lesson's own in `src/lesson/lesson<N>/`. A scene with variants declares them in `scenes.json` (`variants`: the content key, the allowed values, each one's extra content and the order its events must come in), and the props script checks them. Anchors and content a take may not say go under `optional` (the Satoshi panel's month and closing question): the scene shows them only when the config gives them, so no stand-in is needed. The face point the punch-ins and the bubble centre on is the config's `face` (default 840, 450), set by `make_lesson_base.py frame` in `investing-bros-crypto-course`. A `bubble` scene draws the whole frame behind Amitai's circle; a `side` scene draws inside `SidePanel`. A `full` scene draws cards on the wall to his left while he stays on camera, above his video and under the captions (`wall-card`: cards that replace each other, each a number or phrase with a line under it, an optional kicker strip, or a `pair` drawn as a ≠ b; `tokenize`: assets that come in on their names and turn into gold tokens; both in `WallCards.tsx`, 16:9 only). A `full` scene may start in the first 30 s, like the name strips; keep it off the name and title strips' moments, since both use the same wall. Shared pieces (strips, panel, entrance springs) are in `src/lesson/kit.tsx` and `panels.tsx`.

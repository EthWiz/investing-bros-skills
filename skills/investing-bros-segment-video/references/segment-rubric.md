# Segment rubric: high convexity, good flow (Amitai, 2026-11-05)

Score every candidate segment **before cutting it**, from the transcript (and a quick
listen of its first 30 s). Each criterion 0–2. Fast enough to do out loud on a call.

| # | Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook (first 30 s)** | warm-up, logistics, "so..." | topic named, no tension | a question, number or surprise said on camera |
| 2 | **Convexity (payoff ÷ time)** | answer is vague or obvious | one solid takeaway | a payoff far bigger than the minutes spent (a number, a rule, a reversal) |
| 3 | **Momentum / flow** | long pauses, tangents, low energy | some dead stretches | builds steadily, energetic speakers, few dead moments |
| 4 | **Clean open/close + handoff** | starts mid-thought or needs prior context | small context gap | opens cold, ends on a payoff line, hands off cleanly to the full episode |
| 5 | **Short-ability** | no quotable moment | a 30–60 s moment that needs setup | a 30–60 s moment that stands alone as a Short |
| 6 | **Tie to the episode's core theme** | off-theme | related | the episode's central question, searchable on its own |

**Cut it when:** total ≥ **9/12**, **Hook ≥ 1**, and no criterion is 0. Below that, pick
the next candidate or offer it as the alternative. Write the scores in
`youtube-סגמנט-NN.md` (one line: `Rubric: H2 C2 M1 O2 S2 T2 = 11/12`) and in the hand-back.

## Worked example: episode 10 (Berkshire Hathaway)
| Candidate | H | C | M | O | S | T | Total | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Buffett origin, "איך 1,000 דולר הפכו ל-61 מיליון" (12:24–18:16) | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | **12** | picked |
| Economic moat + owner mindset (27:10–33:06) | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 | **7** | rejected by Amitai |

Why: the origin story opens on "let's dive into the core of the episode", runs the
textile-mill mistake straight into $1,000 → $61M and 19.7% vs 10.5% a year (a huge payoff
for 6 min) and has a ready Short (the $61M line). The moat stretch opens mid-sentence
("לגמרי, דרך אגב..."), explains a concept with no single number or reversal, and drifts
into owner mindset, so it scores 1 almost everywhere despite being on-theme.

# Quality checklist

Check the output itself, not just the prompt.

## Before generation
- Correct exact reference and version, requested topic and supplied episode number.
- Exact title (and host credit where the format has one; episode covers have none); no invented quotations or data.
- Appropriate format specification, actual output-size target and reference roles.

## After generation
- Read every Hebrew word letter by letter. Check אמיתי, רסקין and אחים להשקעות.
- Confirm numbers and punctuation read in the right order.
- Compare the headline/number against the supplied copy, not a guessed title.
- Illustrated art: check dominant navy, cream paper and printed mustard. Real-frame
  Shorts: check the brand overlays while preserving natural photo colours.
- Illustrated art: check cutout engraving and room around the subject. Keep paper
  edges restrained in both styles.
- Ensure the topic is evident and the transformation communicates the intended idea.
- For tokenization, distinguish physical paper from digital ownership fragments.
- At roughly 160px wide, show title/main headline and focal object remain identifiable.
  The host credit may be secondary. At full size it must be readable and correct.
- Episode covers: show mark bottom-right, no host strip, no newspaper base, subject big.
- Check safe margins, cropped letters, accidental extra text, invented logos and clutter.
- For edits, compare unchanged regions against the exact source; do not claim perfect
  preservation if generation visibly changed unrelated areas.
- Inspect real dimensions and transparency; do not promise layered or vector files.

## Real-frame Shorts
Apply the frame, layout and pair checks in [Shorts covers](shorts-covers.md). Compare
against the actual extracted frame; do not repair a face or background with imagegen.
Select another real frame for closed eyes or an awkward expression. Verify both
cover files and the recorded host/source timestamp before marking a Short complete.

## Repair
For illustrated artwork, use one localized imagegen edit for an isolated error, then re-inspect. When Hebrew
remains unreliable, propose generating paper/illustration layers and typesetting exact
text with an available authorized layout tool. Do not quietly substitute the wrong name.
Never claim a font is approved or bundled when it is only a proposed candidate.

## Status
Treat square images as selected working references. The two Shorts cover styles have user-selected design guidance, not blanket approval
to publish each output. Treat other formats, actual font
selection and motion/audio as adaptations requiring visual review before they become
new canonical references. User approval of artwork never authorizes external posting.

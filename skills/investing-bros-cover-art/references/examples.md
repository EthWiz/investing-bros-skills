# Thumbnail prompt examples

## Episode 6 — טוקניזציה

Hook: money, stocks and the big banks are moving onto the blockchain (stablecoins, tokenized stocks, BlackRock / JP Morgan, NYSE going 24/7). The published thumbnail used a bitcoin — the episode is barely about bitcoin, so the prompt below shows the real thesis instead.

Headline options (one used marked ✓):
- ✓ `וול סטריט עוברת לבלוקצ'יין`
- `המניה שלך על הבלוקצ'יין?`
- `הכסף עובר לבלוקצ'יין`

Video title stays `פרק 6 - מהפכת הטוקניזציה`, so title and thumbnail say different things.

Accent: **emerald** — money and fintech, matches the show brand, and breaks the run of gold thumbnails (episodes 1, 3, 5 and the published 6).

```
YouTube thumbnail, 16:9, 1280x720, cinematic photoreal, premium finance-podcast style.
One clear focal point in the center: a giant US dollar bill whose right half dissolves into a
stream of glowing digital cubes that link into a chain.
Background: a dark Wall Street skyline at night, softly lit, lots of negative space.
Restrained palette: near-black and deep shadows, white, and a single accent colour, emerald
green, which carries the light on the object, the headline and the pill. No other colours, no
colourful glow, high contrast, shallow depth of field, calm and uncluttered.
Bottom center: huge bold 3D Hebrew headline in white with an emerald glow and a thick black
outline, exactly: "וול סטריט עוברת לבלוקצ'יין".
Under it a small emerald rounded pill reading "פרק 6", and under that small white text
"נדב ואמיתי רסקין".
Leave the top-left corner empty for the channel logo. No other text, no watermarks.
```

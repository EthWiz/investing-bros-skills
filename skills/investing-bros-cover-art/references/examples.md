# Thumbnail prompt examples

## Episode 6 — טוקניזציה

Hook: money, stocks and the big banks are moving onto the blockchain (stablecoins, tokenized stocks, BlackRock / JP Morgan, NYSE going 24/7). The published thumbnail used a bitcoin — the episode is barely about bitcoin, so the prompt below shows the real thesis instead.

Headline options (one used marked ✓):
- ✓ `וול סטריט עוברת לבלוקצ'יין`
- `המניה שלך על הבלוקצ'יין?`
- `הכסף עובר לבלוקצ'יין`

Video title stays `פרק 6 - מהפכת הטוקניזציה`, so title and thumbnail say different things.

```
YouTube thumbnail, 16:9, 1280x720, cinematic photoreal, premium finance-podcast style.
One clear focal point in the center: a giant glowing gold US dollar bill whose right half
dissolves into a stream of shimmering golden digital cubes that link into a glowing chain.
Behind it, a dark Wall Street skyline at night, softly lit.
Dramatic warm gold and amber rim light against deep black and navy shadows, high contrast,
shallow depth of field, no clutter.
Bottom center: huge bold 3D Hebrew headline in metallic gold with a thick black outline and
soft glow, exactly: "וול סטריט עוברת לבלוקצ'יין".
Under it a small gold rounded pill reading "פרק 6", and under that small white text
"נדב ואמיתי רסקין".
Leave the top-left corner empty for the channel logo. No other text, no watermarks.
```

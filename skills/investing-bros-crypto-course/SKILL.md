# Investing Bros crypto course

Load `investing-bros-show` first (naming, Drive tree, approvals) and `investing-bros-brand` for every visual decision. The lesson plan and each lesson's status are in [references/course-plan.md](references/course-plan.md); how a lesson video is made is in [references/video-production.md](references/video-production.md); how the takes are recorded (camera, OBS, mic, framing, background) is in [references/recording-setup.md](references/recording-setup.md).

## When to use
Any work on the crypto course: planning a lesson, its slides, its script, recording and animating its video, filing it in Drive, or changing the course plan. The course is not an episode: it has no `פרק N` folder, RedCircle draft or episode number.

## Inputs
- Lesson number N **as Amitai states it**, and its topic from the course plan. A topic change is Amitai's call; update the plan when he makes it.
- For revisions: the lesson's existing deck link (course plan) or Drive folder.

## Course rules (decided 2026-10-26)
- Free, on the אחים להשקעות channel as one playlist, seven lessons, Hebrew, for Israeli beginners. Risk is covered inside each lesson, not as a lesson of its own. Each lesson stands on its own, since most viewers arrive from search.
- **The stance, the same in every lesson that touches it:** Bitcoin is the base; of all the altcoins, only Ethereum gets a small position; great caution with every other token, and in most cases don't touch it. Present it as Amitai's personal approach.
- **Disclaimer:** in lesson 1 it is the last few sentences of the second beat (decided 2026-10-27), right after why the course matters and who Amitai is: educational content, not investment advice; Amitai isn't a licensed investment adviser; invest only what you can afford to lose. In the video it is spoken with a strip at the bottom of the screen (`תוכן לימודי, לא ייעוץ השקעות`) for those sentences only; the deck keeps it as a slide. Any slide stating the stance carries the line `זו הגישה האישית שלי ולא ייעוץ השקעות מותאם אישית`. Never say "buy now", a target price, or a percentage of anyone's portfolio.
- Facts follow the show's hard rules: every number, date and claim has a source URL in the speaker notes or the script, and Israel-specific facts (tax, exchanges, banks, funds) are checked again before recording because they change.

## Steps
1. **Plan.** Read the lesson's row in the course plan. Write the outline (one sentence per part, 12–16 slides) and confirm it with Amitai before building.
2. **Research.** Collect sources for every fact. If something can't be verified, leave it out or mark it in the speaker notes as unchecked.
3. **Deck.** Build it as a Claude Slides artifact: brand palette and Heebo from `investing-bros-brand`, Hebrew right-to-left as in [references/slides-rtl.md](references/slides-rtl.md), speaker notes in Hebrew on every slide as talking points. Title the deck `קורס קריפטו - שיעור N: <נושא>` (plain hyphen). Add the deck link to the course plan.
4. **Review.** Send Amitai the deck link and apply his changes. The link is private until he shares it from the deck's Share menu.
5. **Drive.** Export the deck as a PDF (the deck page's export, which needs a claude.ai sign-in; if the browser isn't signed in, ask Amitai to sign in or to export it himself) and upload it to the lesson's folder as `מצגת-שיעור-N.pdf`, replacing any older file of that name. Confirm it shows as `application/pdf`.
6. **Script.** When Amitai asks, write the recording script from the deck, split into at most six numbered beats (`קטע N: <נושא> (כ-X שניות)`): one part of the lesson each, usually 60–100 seconds (the opening shorter), recorded as separate takes. Open with a hook, then (in lesson 1) why the course matters and who Amitai is, with the disclaimer at the end of that beat; end with what it gives, a recap and the pointer to the next lesson in one beat; aim for 8 to 10 minutes. Under each beat put an italic `על המסך:` line for the editor (only you, a card beside you, or full screen with you in a corner), then the spoken text. Ask on screen only for what can be built: a scene listed in `investing-bros-remotion`'s `template/src/lesson/scenes.json` or a reusable card, nothing but his face in the first 30 seconds, brand colours only (no red), and no words on a graphic he doesn't say; anything else is a new scene to build before the lesson is cut, so name it as such. End with a sources list. Create it as a Google Doc named `תסריט-שיעור-N` in the lesson's folder (Drive `create_file` from right-to-left HTML), with keep-with-next on each heading and its `על המסך` line. Revise the script in that same Doc, never in a second file: before a big rewrite, ask Amitai to name the current version (File → Version history → Name current version; the connectors can't), so the old text stays a click away and the link never changes.
7. **Record and collect the takes.** Amitai records each beat as its own take, face to camera, in OBS at 1920×1080, 30 fps, as in [references/recording-setup.md](references/recording-setup.md). Takes arrive under his own names (`Beat 1 - take 1.mov`), often in a local `lesson-N/` folder. Check them the moment they arrive with `scripts/check_takes.py lesson-N/lesson-N-video.json --takes-dir DIR --faces` (after Whisper, add `--words-dir`): resolution, clipping, noise floor, the wait before the first word, and framing that drifted between takes; anything it marks FIX is cheapest to fix by recording again now. Framing that drifted is lined up with `make_lesson_base.py frame` (video reference → Workflow). Lesson takes go on the navy studio background (the config's `background`, applied by `make_lesson_base.py build`). With several takes of a beat, use the latest unless he says otherwise. Recording can come in batches: a lesson can be cut from the beats recorded so far.
8. **Read the script Doc.** Before cutting, read the lesson's Google Doc `תסריט-שיעור-N`: its text (Drive `read_file_content`) and its revision (the Docs connector's `read_doc` → `revisionId`; if only Drive metadata is at hand, `get_file_metadata` → `modifiedTime`). It decides what is on screen beat by beat, and Amitai edits it after recording. Save the text, unedited, as `lesson-N/script/תסריט-שיעור-N-<date>.md` and run `scripts/parse_script.py lesson-N/script/… --doc DOC_ID --revision REV --revision-kind revision_id --update lesson-N/lesson-N-video.json`: it writes each beat's title, length, `על המסך` line and text into the config's `script`, records the snapshot and its hash, and lists the beats that changed since the previous snapshot (review those). Where the video deliberately does something else than a beat's `על המסך` line, say why in `script.departures` and in the hand-back. `make_lesson_props.py` refuses a config with no Doc revision, a take with no `על המסך` line, or a snapshot that changed after it was parsed. Which source decides what: [references/video-production.md](references/video-production.md) → Source authority.
9. **Transcribe.** Check the machine first (`scripts/preflight.py --stage transcribe`), then run Whisper on each take, word by word (video reference → Workflow), and stamp the words to their take (`make_lesson_base.py stamp`). The words file per take, `קטע-NN.json`, gives the timing and is never edited; caption wording is the config's `proofread`: what Amitai actually said, not the script. `scripts/draft_proofread.py` drafts it from the script and Whisper and lists every place they disagree; check those by ear.
10. **Animate, render and check.** Each lesson has one config, `lesson-N/lesson-N-video.json` in the lesson's own folder (with its images in `lesson-N/assets/` and script snapshots in `lesson-N/script/`), never in this repo: changing a take, a trim or a caption is an edit there, not a PR. It holds the trims, the proofread words, the anchor phrase for every graphic and the layout; [references/example-lesson-video.json](references/example-lesson-video.json) shows every part. `scripts/make_lesson_base.py` trims and joins the takes, `make_lesson_props.py` and `render_lesson.py` (in `investing-bros-remotion`) build the props and render the 16:9 `Lesson` composition. The words on a graphic go in the config (each segment's `content`), not in the component. Run `scripts/preflight.py --stage render` first; look at the stills and the `--transitions` clip before the full render, and run `check_lesson.py … --record … --manifest WORK/drive/extras/שיעור-N-מניפסט.json` on the render before anyone sees it: it writes the export record only when everything passes and pins the manifest that the metadata bundle will use. To pick up a lesson later, `make_lesson_base.py status` compares the config, takes and words with the last export record and says what to run again. The approach is in the video reference: face first, graphics on the spoken word, one or two custom scenes per lesson plus reusable cards, captions throughout. A new scene is a reusable component in the template (`src/lesson/`, its words and timings from `content`); only a truly one-off scene goes under `src/lesson/lessonN/`. A cut made before every beat is recorded is a draft, `שיעור-N-וידאו-טיוטה.mp4`. Every custom scene is also wanted in 9:16 for Shorts (`double-spend` has it: `render_lesson.py --stills … --vertical`).
11. **File in Drive.** Put in the lesson folder's `extras/`: the takes as `קטע-NN.mp4` (`make_lesson_base.py build --extras … --words-dir WORDS`, a stream copy), the joined base `שיעור-N-בסיס.mp4`, the Whisper words `קטע-NN.json` and their `.take.json` stamps, the captions `שיעור-N-כתוביות.srt` (`make_lesson_props.py --srt`), the scenes' images, the script snapshot, the manifest `שיעור-N-מניפסט.json` (`--extras` writes it: the config plus the skills version and the file names, so `make_lesson_base.py build שיעור-N-מניפסט.json --takes-dir extras/` rebuilds the base, and `make_lesson_props.py` the props, from Drive alone) and the export records `שיעור-N-ייצוא-*.json`. The video (`שיעור-N-וידאו.mp4`, or the draft) goes in the lesson folder itself. Once that checked video and `extras/` have Drive links, run `python3 scripts/export_metadata.py WORK/drive/extras/שיעור-N-מניפסט.json --props WORK/props.json --srt WORK/drive/extras/שיעור-N-כתוביות.srt --record WORK/drive/extras/שיעור-N-ייצוא-<UTC>.json --out VERSION_STAGE/transcript-and-metadata --video-url VIDEO_DRIVE_URL --raw-takes-url EXTRAS_DRIVE_URL`. It refuses a failed or mismatched export record or SRT and verifies the completed editable bundle. Run `python3 scripts/export_metadata.py --verify VERSION_STAGE/transcript-and-metadata` again immediately before upload, then put that whole subfolder in the same Drive folder as that exact checked video version (the lesson folder when it has no version subfolders; otherwise that version's folder), never in `extras/`. Build one bundle per checked draft or final version; never reuse one version's bundle for another or hand-edit generated timing files. For a lesson that predates this rule, list every folder holding a checked video version and backfill each one from its matching manifest, props, SRT and export record; if any matching artifact is unavailable, that version is not done and the hand-back names what is missing. Choosing the upload route: the Drive connector works for the small text files. Videos go with `scripts/drive.sh put FILE FOLDER_ID` when the rclone remote `show` is set up (optional, see `investing-bros-show` → environment). Otherwise, stage the files in the Drive layout in a local `להעלאה-לדרייב/` folder and ask Amitai to drag them in. Confirm afterwards that they are in Drive and read back every file in `transcript-and-metadata/`.
12. **Review and upload.** Amitai reviews the cut; send him a 720p copy under 30 MB if he will watch on his phone (`phone_copy.py` in `investing-bros-remotion`, with a plain ASCII file name; if he hears nothing, it is the phone's silent switch, not the file). Titles, description and upload follow the episode skills he names (`investing-bros-title-description`, `investing-bros-episode-upload`); nothing goes public without his OK. Shorts from the lesson follow `investing-bros-shorts`.

## Done when
- The lesson's folder `קורס קריפטו/שיעור N — נושא/` holds `מצגת-שיעור-N.pdf` (and the Google Doc `תסריט-שיעור-N` when a script was asked for).
- When a video was made: in `extras/` the takes `קטע-NN.mp4`, their Whisper words `קטע-NN.json` with their `.take.json` stamps, the joined base `שיעור-N-בסיס.mp4`, the captions `שיעור-N-כתוביות.srt`, the script snapshot, the manifest `שיעור-N-מניפסט.json` and the export record `שיעור-N-ייצוא-*.json` of the video; the video `שיעור-N-וידאו.mp4` (or the draft `-טיוטה`) in its lesson/version folder; and every folder holding a checked video version has its own `transcript-and-metadata/` with `שיעור-N-תמליל.txt`, `שיעור-N-מילים.json`, the SRT, manifest, `שיעור-N-ציר-זמן.json`, script snapshot, departures, known issues and English `README.md`. Each bundle passes `export_metadata.py --verify`, includes Drive links to that exact video version and its raw takes, and every file is editable plain text, JSON, Markdown or SRT. Its Shorts are in `Shorts/`. Each file counts as filed only once it has been read back from Drive. A new reusable scene is merged in this repo; the lesson's config is not.
- The course plan lists the lesson's deck and script links and current status.

## Hand back
The deck link, the Drive folder link, the sources used, any fact left unchecked, and anything about the right-to-left layout not yet checked in the rendered deck. For a video: its length, the takes' resolution, which beats are in it, the Doc revision it follows and its departures from the Doc, the `check_lesson.py` result and the export record (marked draft when the skills checkout had local changes), the `transcript-and-metadata/` verification result and links recorded in its README, the stills sheet, any words in the captions you couldn't confirm from the audio, and which files are in Drive (or still waiting to be dragged in).

## Never
- Recommend a specific amount, percentage, entry price or timing.
- Present any token other than Bitcoin and Ethereum as something to buy.
- Put English words inside a Hebrew sentence on a slide (see the RTL reference).
- Make the lesson video by presenting the deck slide by slide, or open it with a full-screen graphic in the first 30 seconds.
- Put text on a lesson graphic that Amitai doesn't say, or a number he doesn't say. The one exception: a document he describes, shown as a page (the white paper's title, the text in the first block), in its original language.
- Create a second `קורס קריפטו` folder or a second folder for the same lesson; find them by the `שיעור N` prefix.

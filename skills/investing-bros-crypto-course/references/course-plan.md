# Course plan

Seven lessons (decided 2026-10-26, after comparing other intro courses with Amitai's earlier Crypto 101 deck), about 12–20 minutes each, one a week on a fixed day apart from the podcast, as the playlist `קורס קריפטו`. Risk has no lesson of its own: each lesson covers the risks of its own topic. Each lesson ends by pointing to the next one and asking viewers to subscribe and leave questions in the comments; the best questions are answered in a podcast episode. Each lesson is recorded face to camera in beats with Remotion animations on top ([video-production.md](video-production.md)); its custom animations, built in 9:16 too, give two or three Shorts per lesson.

Drive: [`אחים להשקעות/קורס קריפטו/`](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1NEF6tCwkr0J8MGnZ3gvV7ApyxeUn6SA7), one folder per lesson. The Google Doc [`קורס קריפטו - תוכנית השיעורים`](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1TcP7sPoFgE5qKawVnkrr9Bmk1HBFE-hVjrnvY6INkZ4/edit) at the folder's root is the plan Amitai and Nadav read; keep it and this table in step.

Amitai's Crypto 101 deck (Canva, "Crypto 101 - עמותה") is source material for every lesson. Its facts need checking before reuse: Ethereum launched in July 2015 (the deck says 2016), the founder is Vitalik Buterin, and its market-size numbers (Bitcoin vs gold, stablecoins) are out of date.

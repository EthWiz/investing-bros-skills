# Course plan

Five lessons, about 12–18 minutes each, one a week on a fixed day apart from the podcast, as the playlist `קורס קריפטו`. Each lesson ends by pointing to the next one and asking viewers to subscribe and leave questions in the comments; the best questions are answered in a podcast episode. Two or three Shorts per lesson are planned; how they are made is not decided yet.

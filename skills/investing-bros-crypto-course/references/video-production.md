# Lesson video: beats, face first, Remotion on top

Decided with Amitai on 2026-10-26 for lesson 1, and the default for every lesson. Motion and rendering belong to `investing-bros-remotion`; colours, type and artwork to `investing-bros-brand`. This page says how a lesson video is put together.

## The approach
- **Amitai's face carries the lesson; graphics punctuate it.** The video is not the deck presented slide by slide. The deck stays a handout in the lesson folder; the video is built from the script.
- **Nothing static for long.** No more than about 12 seconds on screen without a change: a graphic coming in, a reveal, a punch-in or a cut. Word-timed reveals give most of these for free.
- **The first 30 seconds are face only.** No full-screen graphic there; a graphic at 0:05 reads as a webinar. The lesson title shows for 3 seconds at most.
- **The disclaimer is a few spoken sentences** (about 10 seconds) at the end of a beat, not a beat of its own; in lesson 1, the end of beat 2, after the why and who Amitai is. A strip at the bottom of the screen, `תוכן לימודי, לא ייעוץ השקעות`, covers those sentences. No full disclaimer slide in the video.
- **No agenda slide.** Say what the lesson covers in one sentence, show the parts as small cards for a few seconds, then keep them as a progress marker in a corner for the whole lesson (one mark per part).
- Length: about 10 minutes, 8 to 12 at most. A lesson that runs over is split, not sped up.

## Beats
A beat is one part of the lesson, usually 60 to 100 seconds (the opening shorter), recorded as its own take; at most six beats a lesson (decided 2026-10-27, after lesson 1's 14 short beats felt chopped up and took long to record). Inside a beat, graphics still change every few sentences on his words. The script (SKILL.md step 6) is written in numbered beats, each with an `על המסך:` line that picks one of these:

| On screen | When | Remotion |
|---|---|---|
| Only Amitai | Opening, who Amitai is and the disclaimer, summary, any beat that is an argument rather than a mechanism | `full`, with punch-ins; strips on the wall |
| A card beside him, his face stays in view | Definitions, short lists, the end card: terms or items appear one at a time on the word that names them | `side` with a scene in the panel |
| Full screen, his face in a small corner bubble | Mechanisms that need a picture: how money moves today, double spending, the shared ledger, the chain, consensus | `bubble` with a scene |
| Background footage | Dates and history, such as 2009 and Satoshi: headlines, the first page of a document | `side`; documents drawn in the scene, no stock footage |

## Source authority
Each source decides one thing. When two disagree, this table says which one wins.

| Source | Decides |
|---|---|
| Course rules and plan (SKILL.md, `course-plan.md`) | Audience, curriculum, the stance, what each lesson is |
| The script Doc, as of the snapshot the config names | The intended text, and each beat's `על המסך` direction |
| The selected take | What Amitai said |
| Whisper's words `קטע-NN.json` | Timing only: when each word is said. Never edited |
| The config's `proofread` | Caption and card wording: what he said, corrected by ear |
| The rest of the lesson config | Takes, trims, layout, each scene's words, and `script.departures` |
| The export record and `check_lesson.py`'s report | What produced a given export and what was checked |

- When the take differs from the script, captions follow the take. What is on screen follows the Doc, unless `script.departures` says why not. No graphic states something he didn't say.
- A word that can't be confirmed from the audio goes in the config's `notes` and the hand-back. It never becomes a caption or a graphic without a mark.
- When the Doc changes, save a new snapshot and parse it: `parse_script.py` lists the beats whose text or `על המסך` line changed, and only those are reviewed. An older export keeps the snapshot it was made from.
- A passing check and an export record are not Amitai's approval. His OK is in the hand-back.

## Workflow
Each lesson has one config, `lesson-N/lesson-N-video.json` in the lesson's own folder, beside its images (`lesson-N/assets/`) and script snapshots (`lesson-N/script/`). It holds the script Doc revision it follows, the takes and their trims, the proofread words of each take, an anchor phrase for every graphic, the layout with each scene's words, and the scenes' images with their sources. It is never merged into this repo; [example-lesson-video.json](example-lesson-video.json) is a made-up lesson that shows every part. Paths in it are relative to the config, so the folder can move. Locally it is the working copy; the Drive package (`extras/`) is the shared copy another agent or machine resumes from. Paths below: `CC` = this skill's `scripts/`, `RM` = `investing-bros-remotion`'s `scripts/`, `WORK` = a work folder such as `lesson-N/work/`.

0. **Preflight** before a long stage: `python3 CC/preflight.py --stage transcribe|base|render|deliver [--fonts FONTS --logo LOGO]`. It checks the tools, models, fonts and disk the stage needs. A missing upload route is only reported, so a local render isn't blocked.
1. **Script in beats** (SKILL.md step 6). Read it aloud once and time it before recording.
2. **Read the Doc into the config** (SKILL.md step 8): save the Doc's text as `lesson-N/script/תסריט-שיעור-N-<date>.md`, then `python3 CC/parse_script.py lesson-N/script/… --doc DOC_ID --revision REVISION_ID --revision-kind revision_id --update lesson-N/lesson-N-video.json`. The `על המסך` lines decide the layout; a deliberate difference goes in `script.departures` with the reason.
3. **Record each beat as a separate take**, face to camera with a teleprompter, in OBS at 1080p30 with the setup in [recording-setup.md](recording-setup.md) (Center Stage off, the PD100X only, the framing guide). A stumble costs one beat, not the lesson. At 1080p, punch-ins stay at 1.2x at most; lesson 1's first takes came in 720p and were upscaled. When the takes arrive, `python3 CC/check_takes.py lesson-N/lesson-N-video.json --takes-dir DIR --faces` reports resolution, clipping, the noise floor and framing drift (lesson 1's OBS beats: faces 617–714 px wide, centres 784–969 px across, room noise at −45 dB).
4. **Trims.** `python3 CC/make_lesson_base.py suggest lesson-N/lesson-N-video.json --takes-dir DIR --words-dir WORK/words` prints a from/to per take: 0.3 s before the first word, 0.45 s after the last. Without the words it listens for silence instead (ignoring the click of the camera starting and stopping), which room noise can defeat: lesson 1's OBS takes came back whole, so it now warns. Put them in the config. A take can only be trimmed at its ends: a restart in the middle stays in the audio (lesson 1 beat 5), and the caption shows the clean sentence with a note.
5. **Words.** Transcribe each whole take (they are short) with Whisper, word by word:
   `whisper קטע-NN.mp4 --model large-v3-turbo --model_dir ~/.cache/whisper --language he --word_timestamps True --output_format json --condition_on_previous_text False --fp16 False --output_dir WORK/words`
   (the Homebrew `whisper` command on Amitai's Mac; elsewhere `transcribe_clip.py` in `investing-bros-shorts` writes a format the props script also reads). Six takes, 3.5 minutes of speech, took about 10 minutes on the Mac's CPU. Whisper makes up words over silence at the end of a take ("תודה רבה."): trim them out. Then stamp each words file to its take: `python3 CC/make_lesson_base.py stamp lesson-N/lesson-N-video.json --takes-dir DIR --words-dir WORK/words` writes `קטע-NN.take.json` (the take's name and sha256), so words from a take that was later replaced are refused instead of silently mistimed.
6. **Proofread** each take into the config's `proofread`: what Amitai said, not the script. `python3 CC/draft_proofread.py lesson-N/lesson-N-video.json --words-dir WORK/words [--write]` drafts it: the script's spelling where Whisper garbles a word, his ad-libs added, skipped phrases dropped, and every disagreement listed to check by ear. He departs from it ("או הבנק המרכזי", "קוראים לזה אינפלציה", "מילה שנייה היא קריפטו"), and captions and card text follow what he said. Where the audio can't be checked and Whisper is garbled, mark the guess in the config's `notes` and in the hand-back.
7. **Base.** `python3 CC/make_lesson_base.py build lesson-N/lesson-N-video.json --takes-dir DIR -o WORK/base.mp4 --extras WORK/drive/extras --words-dir WORK/words` trims the takes, scales them to 1920×1080, joins them in beat order and levels the audio to -14 LUFS; `--extras` also writes the Drive package: the takes as `קטע-NN.mp4`, the scenes' images, the script snapshot, the words with their stamps (each stamp also gets the Drive copy's sha256, since the remux has different bytes) and the manifest `שיעור-N-מניפסט.json` (the config plus the skills version and the file names). The manifest is itself a config: `build שיעור-N-מניפסט.json --takes-dir extras/` rebuilds the same base from Drive, since a take is found by its own name or as `קטע-NN.mp4`, and `make_lesson_props.py שיעור-N-מניפסט.json --words-dir extras/` the same props. With a `background` block (`{"method": "vision", "image": "assets/lesson-background-navy-studio.jpg"}`, the image copied from `investing-bros-brand`), `build` also puts Amitai on that background in every take: `vision` makes an Apple Vision matte per take first (macOS, `swiftc`; about 40 s per 25 s of take, cached in `WORK/mattes/` by the take's hash), `chroma` keys out the green screen (`key`: color, similarity, blend). The takes in `extras/` stay the originals and the image goes in the package, so a rebuild from Drive keys them again; a changed background makes `status` ask for base, props and render. When the takes are framed differently, `python3 CC/make_lesson_base.py frame lesson-N/lesson-N-video.json --takes-dir DIR` (after the `background` block) measures the face in each take and writes a `frame` per take and the config's `face`: every take is scaled to the same face width and centred at x 960 (the brand background's glow), its shoulders kept on the bottom edge, a take with the head cut off never shrunk and none moved up (lesson_framing.py). Re-run it after replacing a take. Levelling runs over the whole joined base, so adding a beat can shift the others' loudness slightly; `check_lesson.py` checks the result. No speed change.
8. **Props.** `python3 RM/make_lesson_props.py lesson-N/lesson-N-video.json --words-dir WORK/words --takes-dir DIR -o WORK/props.json --srt WORK/drive/extras/שיעור-N-כתוביות.srt`. It aligns the proofread words to Whisper's timings, makes two-line captions that keep sentences whole, resolves every anchor phrase to the moment it is said, and refuses a phrase it can't find, an unknown scene or one in the wrong mode, a scene missing an anchor, a content key or an image its entry in the template's `src/lesson/scenes.json` asks for (or with one timed outside its segment), segments out of order or past the end, a full-screen or side graphic in the first 30 s, a punch-in over 1.25x, a config with no script Doc revision or whose snapshot changed after parsing, a scene variant that isn't allowed or has its events out of order (double-spend's `scenario`), and words stamped to a different take. Re-timing a graphic = changing its phrase or offset in the config; changing a graphic's words = its segment's `content`. A phrase said twice in a beat is warned about: the first is used, so add a word to pick the other. A scene's `optional` anchors and content (scenes.json) may be left out when the take doesn't say them.
9. **Stills, then render.** `python3 RM/render_lesson.py --props WORK/props.json --base WORK/base.mp4 --fonts FONTS --logo LOGO --out WORK/lesson.mp4 --stills 25,38,50,64,90,100,120,140,160,180` (Heebo TTFs from `brand.py fonts` in `investing-bros-shorts`, the logo from `investing-bros-brand`). Check the sheet: captions under Amitai, nothing over his face, every card's text readable, the panel edges flush with the frame. Then `--transitions` (the same command): one clip of 1 s before to 1.5 s after every cut and layout change, with sound, to watch whether each lands on the word; stills can't show timing. Then the same command with neither, in the background (3:16 took 8.5 minutes on 10 cores). A later change to the config (a chart, a caption, an anchor) needs only `--patch` on the same command: it re-renders just the stretches that changed and splices them into the last render; check that render's exit code before `check_lesson.py`, since a failed render leaves the old video in place. It lines the sound up with the base afterwards (Remotion's AAC played it 43 ms late); lesson 1's 7:16 took 10 minutes. On Amitai's Mac the system Python lacks numpy and Pillow: run the RM scripts as `uv run --with numpy --with pillow python …`. Write render commands out in full: zsh doesn't split a command kept in a variable, so `$R --stills …` runs nothing. Last, `python3 RM/check_lesson.py WORK/lesson.mp4 --props WORK/props.json --base WORK/base.mp4 --fonts FONTS --record WORK/drive/extras --config lesson-N/lesson-N-video.json --takes-dir DIR --manifest WORK/drive/extras/שיעור-N-מניפסט.json` must print OK: format, length, loudness, sync at every cut, dead air at a cut, captions in order and on two lines at most. Only then it writes the export record `שיעור-N-ייצוא-<UTC>.json` (never over an older one): the video's hash and format, the report, the hashes of the config, props, base, manifest, script snapshot and takes, and the skills commit; `draft: true` when the skills checkout had local changes, so render from committed code. A scene that also works in 9:16 is checked with `--stills … --vertical` (the `LessonScenes` composition, the scenes alone on navy).
10. **Resume.** Before picking a lesson up again, `python3 CC/make_lesson_base.py status lesson-N/lesson-N-video.json --takes-dir DIR --words-dir WORK/words --record WORK/drive/extras` compares the takes, words, config and script with the last export record and says what to run again: a new take means Whisper, proofread, base, props and render; new trims or framing mean base, props and render; new proofread, anchors or layout mean props and render; a new script snapshot means review the changed beats. It runs nothing, and nothing counts as done because a status line says so: the files and their hashes decide.
11. **File and review.** Drive files as in SKILL.md step 11. After the checked video and raw-takes `extras/` folder have Drive links, build `VERSION_STAGE/transcript-and-metadata/` with `python3 CC/export_metadata.py` from that export's manifest, props, SRT and export record. It converts the aligned proofread caption words to `(beat, start, end, word)` on the final timeline, maps every raw take trim to final time, carries over every resolved scene/layout stretch and overlay anchor, and copies the script, departures, notes and warnings. It refuses a failed or mismatched record, manifest or SRT and verifies every file is documented editable UTF-8; run its `--verify` mode again before upload. Put it in the same Drive folder as that exact checked video version, not in `extras/` and not beside another version. Amitai reviews the cut (a 720p copy under 30 MB for his phone, the full file can't be sent there: `python3 RM/phone_copy.py WORK/lesson.mp4 -o WORK/lesson-N-phone-720p.mp4`, an ASCII name, and it checks the sound). Nothing goes public without his OK.

## Layout (lesson 1, the default)
Amitai sits in the middle of the frame, with the space to his left free: the navy studio background since 2026-10-28 (a real wall before). The composition (`src/lesson/` in the template) does the rest:
- **Face position:** punch-ins and the corner bubble centre on the face at x 840, y 450 of the base (between the eyes and the mouth). When a recording setup frames him elsewhere, give the base's point as `"face": {"x": …, "y": …}` in the config; the bubble never shows past the bottom of the frame.
- **Only Amitai (`full`):** zoom 1 is the wide shot. Punch-ins of 1.1–1.2x are hard cuts at a sentence start (`[beat, "first words", -0.15]`), so the face beats change every 5–15 s. Use zoom 1 while something sits on the wall (name, title, agenda).
- **Name and title:** the name strips (`אמיתי רסקין` / `אחים להשקעות`) when he says his name; the title strips (`שיעור N` + the name as he says it) for 3 s at most, near the end of the opening beat. Both on the wall to his left.
- **Disclaimer:** the strip `תוכן לימודי, לא ייעוץ השקעות` at the bottom from the disclaimer's first words (`disclaimer_from: [beat, "phrase"]` in the config) to the end of `disclaimer_beat`; without `disclaimer_from` it covers the whole beat. Captions move up above it.
- **Agenda:** the parts come in on the wall one by one as he names them (`ag1`, `ag2`, …), then shrink into the progress marker top left (the top right is his head once he moves). The marker shows the current part by name for the rest of the lesson.
- **Card beside him (`side`):** he slides right and a navy panel with a torn edge comes in from the left, one beat long. Scenes inside it change on his words.
- **Full screen (`bubble`):** the scene covers the frame and Amitai sits in a gold-ringed circle bottom right, on his side; flows run right to left, starting from him.
- **Captions:** two calm lines at the bottom, the word being said in gold, under Amitai: centred in `full`, on his side in `side`, left of the circle in `bubble`.

## Animations: build few, reuse them
- **Per lesson, one or two custom animations** for the beats that teach, and everything else from a small set of reusable cards (a term card, a row of items that tick on, an end card). Animating every beat in every lesson doesn't ship.
- **Build each custom animation as one component with props**, so it can play twice with a different ending. Lesson 1: the double-spend animation plays once as the problem (the shekel reaches Dani and Yossi) and once with the shared ledger behind it (the second payment is rejected).
- **Right to left.** Graphics enter from the right, and flows (money moving, steps, a chain) run right to left, Amitai's side on the right. Hebrew in Heebo; brand colours from `investing-bros-brand`.
- **Every custom animation in 16:9 and 9:16** from the start, so it doubles as a Short: two or three Shorts per lesson. The scene reads the frame size and lays itself out (`double-spend` does; mark it `"vertical": true` in `scenes.json`). Shorts then follow `investing-bros-shorts`. (`lesson1-money-flow` and `lesson1-satoshi` exist in 16:9 only so far.)
- **Build for the beats that are recorded.** Plan the custom scenes from the script, but build first the ones whose beats are in hand: a scene for a beat not yet recorded can't be timed or checked.
- **A document Amitai talks about may appear as a page** (the white paper's first page, the text Satoshi left in the first block), in its own language: the real page as an image when there is one (lesson 1's white paper page is rendered from bitcoin.org's PDF, its source and hash in the config), otherwise drawn in the composition. That is the only English on a lesson graphic, and the only text he doesn't say word for word.

Lesson 1 so far: a draft cut from beats 1–6 of the earlier 14-beat script, with the money moving through the intermediaries (`lesson1-money-flow`, full screen), 2008 and Satoshi (`lesson1-satoshi`, side panel) and the three words (the reusable `terms` card, side panel). These scenes carry over to the six-beat script (beats 3 and 5). Double spending (beat 4) is built as the reusable `double-spend` scene: `scenario: "copy"` for the problem, then `scenario: "ledger"` from "הרעיון" (the bank's ledger becoming everyone's copy, the second payment refused), in 16:9 and 9:16; it is timed once beat 4 is recorded. Its X is the brand's hook yellow, not red: the palette has no red, so the Doc's "איקס אדום" is a departure to record for that beat. Beat 2's comparison is the reusable `growth-bars` scene (Bitcoin against the S&P 500, gold and the TA-125 on one scale) and its 2018 drop the reusable `price-drop` scene, both full screen. Beat 4's trap (two computers that heard the payments in a different order) is the reusable `conflict` scene; beat 5 is the reusable `mining` scene (a simplified mining setup) and `chain` scene (a changed old page breaks its link); beat 6's private key is the reusable `private-key` scene, all full screen (version 4, 2026-10-29).

## Files
In the lesson's Drive folder:
- the lesson folder itself: the video `שיעור-N-וידאו.mp4`, or `שיעור-N-וידאו-טיוטה.mp4` for a cut made before every beat is recorded;
- `extras/`: the takes `קטע-NN.mp4` (NN = the beat number in the script), their Whisper words `קטע-NN.json` and stamps `קטע-NN.take.json`, the joined base `שיעור-N-בסיס.mp4`, the captions `שיעור-N-כתוביות.srt`, the scenes' images, the script snapshot, the manifest `שיעור-N-מניפסט.json` and the export records `שיעור-N-ייצוא-*.json` (one per checked export), so the next pass can start from Drive without transcribing again;
- beside each checked video version, `transcript-and-metadata/`: that version's editable final-timeline `שיעור-N-תמליל.txt`, `שיעור-N-מילים.json`, SRT, manifest, `שיעור-N-ציר-זמן.json`, script snapshot, script departures, known issues/caption caveat, and an English `README.md` documenting every file, JSON schema, time base and Drive link to that exact video and its raw takes;
- `Shorts/`: Shorts made from the lesson.

The lesson's config, its images and its script snapshots live in the lesson's own folder and reach Drive inside the manifest package; only reusable scenes (components in the template) are merged into this repo.
